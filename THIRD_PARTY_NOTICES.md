# Third-party material

The piano recordings embedded in films/window-seat/index.html (the "piano-bank"
JSON block) and present in the audio of any export of that film are derived from
Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They are
not covered by the MIT license in LICENSE. Source, pinned commit and modifications are
listed in films/window-seat/AUDIO-SOURCES.md. Keep that attribution when
redistributing the film, its piano bank or its exports.

The wing geometry in films/eclosion/index.html (the `WING` constant: outlines, colour
cells and spots) was measured by colour thresholds from "Danaus plexippus MHNT dos.jpg"
by Didier Descouens (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Danaus_plexippus_MHNT_dos.jpg),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), then smoothed and completed by hand.
Whether that license reaches measured outlines of a natural pattern has not been
established; the data is credited and offered under the same license to be safe, and is
not covered by the MIT license in LICENSE. films/eclosion/measure-wings.py rebuilds it
from the hash-pinned photograph.
